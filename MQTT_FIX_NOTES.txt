Overdrive BYD v1.0.2

- Supports legacy JSON payload on overdrive/vehicle/telemetry.
- Supports current per-key topics on overdrive/vehicle/telemetry/+.
- Normalizes location {latitude, longitude} to lat/lon for the device tracker.
- Filters known BYD sentinel values such as 1048575 and -10011.
- Fixes all entities becoming unavailable when the availability topic is absent, non-retained, or encoded as 1/0 or true/false.
- Live telemetry now proves availability and restores the vehicle automatically.
- MQTT byte payloads are decoded correctly before JSON/number parsing.
